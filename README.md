# F&B-Analysis-Dashboard
Excited to share my latest Power BI project – Luxury Hospitality F&B Sales Performance Dashboard! 📊

I’m happy to showcase an interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard that I developed as part of my learning journey, designed to transform F&B sales data into meaningful business insights.

🔎 Project Highlights

✅ Executive Overview Dashboard
✅ F&B Sales Performance Analysis
✅ Outlet-wise Revenue Analysis
✅ Revenue & Covers Analysis
✅ Resident vs Non-Resident Analysis
✅ Food, Beverage & Liquor Analysis
✅ Budget vs Actual Performance
✅ KPI Tracking & Interactive Filters

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

> Power BI
> Power Query
> DAX
> Data Modeling
> Data Visualization
> Business Intelligence

📈 Key Insights

> Revenue increased from ₹26.87 Cr to ₹60.16 Cr.
> Food was the largest revenue contributor.
> Avartana & Peshawri were the leading outlets.
> Non-residents contributed the larger share of revenue.
> Dinner generated the highest revenue among shifts.
> CPD & ARC showed positive improvement.

💡 Learning & Takeaway

This project helped me strengthen my skills in Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data modeling, and dashboard development, while improving my understanding of how business data can be converted into actionable insights.

📌 Note:
This is a self-created portfolio project developed for learning and practice purposes using sample data. It does not contain any confidential or company data.
